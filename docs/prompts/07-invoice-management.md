# Prompt 07 — Invoice Management

> **Source:** Extracted verbatim from the master prompt supplied on 2026-10-20
> ("PHASE 8 — Invoice Management").

Create a polished invoice list and management view.

Include:

- Invoice number.
- Customer name.
- Issue date.
- Due date.
- Grand total.
- Status.
- Search by customer or invoice number.
- Filter by status.
- Open and edit actions.
- Delete with confirmation.
- Create New Invoice action.

Use clear status badges and helpful empty states.

Ensure navigation between the list, editor, and preview works.

Document and commit.
