# Prompt 02 — Invoice Editor and Live Preview

> **Source:** Extracted verbatim from the master prompt supplied on 2026-10-20
> ("PHASE 3 — Invoice Editor and Live Preview").

Implement editable fields for:

## Business details

- Business name.
- Business email.
- Business phone.
- Business address.
- Website.
- Optional tax registration number.
- Optional business logo.

## Customer details

- Customer name.
- Customer email.
- Customer phone.
- Billing address.
- Optional company name.

## Invoice metadata

- Invoice number, automatically generated but editable.
- Issue date.
- Due date.
- Currency fixed to USD for this assessment.
- Optional payment terms.
- Status: Draft, Saved, Sent.

## Line items

Each line item must support:

- Description.
- Quantity.
- Unit price.
- Calculated amount.
- Add.
- Edit.
- Remove.

The live invoice preview must immediately reflect all edits.

Use sensible demo values. Do not send emails automatically.

Document and commit the phase.
